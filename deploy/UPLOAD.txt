EVALUATOR - upload to evaluator.watch
=====================================

1. Unzip evaluator-watch.zip OR copy all files from this folder
2. Put them in the web root of evaluator.watch
3. Keep the folder structure (assets/, data/ next to index.html)

Required at site root:
  index.html
  hot.html
  styles.css
  app.js
  hot.js
  theme.js
  cursor.js
  assets/
  data/

Do not upload: scripts/, docs/, README.md, .git
